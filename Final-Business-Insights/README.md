# Final Business Insights

The analysis of the café transaction dataset produced several useful business insights.

## 1. Product Performance

Salad recorded the highest sales among the analysed products, with sales of ₹16,605. This indicates strong demand for the product and suggests that the café should maintain sufficient availability of high-performing products.

## 2. Overall Sales Performance

The dataset contains 10,000 transactions with total sales of approximately ₹84,763.50. This provides an overall view of the sales performance represented in the dataset.

## 3. Customer Payment Preferences

The analysis identified differences in the usage of payment methods. Understanding customer payment preferences can help the café ensure that commonly used payment options remain easily available.

## 4. Location Performance

The analysis showed differences in transaction activity across locations. Comparing location-level performance can help the business identify stronger-performing areas and areas that may require additional promotional attention.

## 5. Monthly Sales Trends

Monthly sales varied across the analysed period. Monitoring these patterns can help the café plan inventory, staffing, and promotional activities according to changes in demand.

## 6. Data-Driven Decision Making

The combination of data cleaning, exploratory analysis, and dashboard visualization provides a structured way to understand the café's transaction data and supports more informed business decisions.

## Final Recommendation

The café can use the findings from this analysis to monitor high-performing products, understand customer payment behaviour, compare location performance, and plan business activities based on historical sales patterns.
